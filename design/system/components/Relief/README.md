# Relief

The routed-ridge motif: a crisp crest line with a lit flank tapering away toward the top left and a shaded flank toward the bottom right, like contour lines pressed into slate. PageHeader and EmptyState use it.

Provide `variant` (`route` or `hairpin`), and optionally `width`, `height`, `preserve`, `className` and `fade` (false keeps the lanes at full strength to the edge of their box).

- Decorative only (`aria-hidden`). Use once per view, never behind or under text, never animated.
- Intentional addition: the brief names no motif component, and this one carries the visual language.
