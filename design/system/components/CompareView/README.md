# CompareView

Two Ethos runs side by side on the same scenarios: each label's share under prompt A and B, and the difference with its interval on a channel centred at zero.

Provide `a` and `b` (`{name}`), `rows` (`{label, family, a, b, diff, low, high}`, shares and points), and optionally `span` (the axis reach in points, 40 by default).
