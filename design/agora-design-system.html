Motif

Relief

Agora

A calm instrument for seeing how our agents behave.

The motif, in dark and light: flat faces, shoulders lit from the top left, a soft rim, and light falling off onto the ground. Every component on this page is live: sort the tables, switch tabs, hover or tab to the highlights, open the folded messages. Choose Dark, Light or Both at the top left; Both shows each component in the two themes, one above the other. Everything shown is synthetic.

Guidelines

Agora is an internal instrument for seeing how our AI agents behave in health insurance and personal health. It should feel like technological slate: calm, smooth, exact. The one visual idea is the channel, a rounded groove routed through the surface and lit from the top left, taken from the softly bevelled tubes of a dark slate relief. Use it boldly in one place per view (the PageHeader's Relief) and quietly everywhere else (tracks, grooves, wells, the conversation's route). Never use neon, glows of colour, gradients as decoration, or grids of identical cards.

Content fundamentals

  • Write plain British English in sentence case. Short sentences. No exclamation marks, no emoji, no all-caps labels.
  • Name things by what people do: "Start audit of 12 samples", "Download the Inspect log", "Save and label the next". A button's verb returns in its toast: "Cancel run" leads to "Run cancelled".
  • Errors say what happened and how to fix it, without apology: "It used 400,000 of 400,000 tokens. Raise the budget in the launcher and start it again."
  • Empty states invite the next step in the app's own words: "Nothing is waiting for your review." "No open model has read this run yet."
  • Use these words exactly; they come from the data:
  • Run kinds: Explore, Measure, Lens, Playground, Scenario set, Companion.
  • Statuses: queued, running, succeeded, failed, cancelled, stopped at budget.
  • Intensity: absent (0), faint (1), clear (2), dominant (3); a two-judge median may be a half, such as 1.5.
  • Expected profile: present (with a band such as 1 to 2), absent, or either. Fit is a percentage per reply and per transcript.
  • Trust: trusted (agreement 0.8 or more), provisional (from 0.667), retired (below), unrated.
  • Families and labels: Care (emotional reaction, interpretation, exploration); Collaboration (reflection, open question, autonomy support, partnership); Judgement (calibrated uncertainty, scope discipline, referral, privacy care); Curiosity (purposeful question, reflexive question); Risk (sycophancy, false reassurance, over hedging, padding).
  • Review verdicts: agree, disagree (with a corrected value), unsure.
  • Open-model signal kinds: probe, persona vector, autoencoder explanation.
  • Fixed wording, always as written:
  • Lens pages carry "Expressed disposition, read from text. Lens does not see inside the model." (PageHeader note).
  • Thinking summaries are labelled as summaries "of the agent's reasoning, written by a separate model".
  • The open-model pane says its signals are that model's reading of the text, "not the agent's own internals".
  • Reports say that everything in Agora is synthetic.
  • Sample content is always synthetic. The house example: Priya Sandford, member TEST-100231, Gold cover, knee MRI claim CLM-TEST-5521 declined, seed declined-mri-promise, scenario type claim_declined_distress, Claims assistant prompt v12.

Visual foundations

Light and bezels. One light source, top left, in both themes, and every edge answers it with a falloff, never a flat drop shadow. - Raised controls (buttons, the selected tab, the active nav item, checked boxes) have a 1px bezel outline running from color-bezel-hi on top to color-bezel-lo below, a face that is lighter on top, and shadow-raise (a lit inner top line, then a soft falloff beneath). Hovering brightens the bezel toward color-sheen. Pressing reverses it: the bezel and face flip and the control sinks into shadow-inset. - Grooves (tab and nav bars, tracks) run the other way: color-bezel-lo on the upper wall, color-bezel-hi on the lower lip, a color-channel floor with shadow-inset falling off from the top. - Containers (cards, tables, dialogs, toasts, the agent's messages) catch the light on their top edge: their border runs from a mix of color-bezel-hi into color-border by 42% of their height. - Fields keep a solid color-border-strong edge so they meet 3:1, with the well's falloff and lip inside it.

Themes. Dark (slate) is the first theme and the fallback; light (mist) mirrors it. Set data-theme="dark" or "light" on html. Both are first-class: check every new pairing in both.

Surfaces. The page is color-bg with a faint radial luminance from the top left toward color-surface. Content sits on color-surface; quiet fills use color-surface-muted. Separate regions with tone and space first, color-border hairlines second. Cards (radius-md, a color-rim top edge) group content; they are not the default container.

Colour. - Text is color-text; secondary text color-text-muted. Both hold 4.5:1 on color-bg, color-surface, color-surface-muted, the status -bg tints and every family tint up to intensity-3, in both themes. - color-accent is a pale steel in dark and a deep slate blue in light: primary buttons, links, the selected state. Text on it is color-accent-text. - Status: color-ok, color-warn, color-danger, each on its own -bg tint. Every status carries a word and a glyph; colour never carries it alone. - Lens families (family-care, family-collaboration, family-judgement, family-curiosity, family-risk) follow Okabe and Ito's colour-blind-safe set, muted for slate. They only group: the label name always shows beside the hue. - Intensity shows as a family tint: color-mix(in srgb, var(--family-…) var(--intensity-n), transparent). intensity-1 18%, intensity-2 30%, intensity-3 42%; absent shows none. (The brief's 20, 35 and 50% left text under 4.5:1 on dominant curiosity in dark.) - color-expected is the faint lit band behind ProfileStrip tracks. color-signal is a silver, achromatic scale for open-model signals, kept apart from the five family hues. - Charts use color-chart-1 for the single series and color-chart-grid for rules. A worse version switches to color-danger and says so in words. - Focus is a solid 2px color-focus outline, 2px off the control, 3:1 on every surface. Control edges use color-border-strong (3:1).

Type. Instrument Sans for everything people read; Spline Sans Mono only for hashes, IDs, model names and message references (M3). Page titles (page-title, text-xl) run at font-stretch: 88% for a precise, instrument-like headline; that condensed title is the typographic signature, so use it nowhere else. Body is text-md at leading 1.55, reading lines under 72 characters. Numbers in tables and charts use tabular figures. No all-caps, no eyebrow labels above headings.

Spacing and layout. Steps of 4: space-1 to space-7 (4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32, 48). The main column grows to content-width (72rem) with space-6 gutters. Left-align text; right-align numbers.

Radii. radius-pill for anything that moves or holds a value (buttons, badges, chips, tabs, nav, every channel), echoing the rounded ends of the routed channels. radius-md for containers (cards, fields, tables, notices; dialogs add 4px). radius-sm for small inline marks (Mono, citations, highlights, checkboxes).

Elevation. shadow-1 only for dialogs and toasts. Everything else is either raised a hair (shadow-raise) or set into a channel (shadow-inset). In CSS, a bezel is a transparent 1px border with two background layers: the face on padding-box, the bezel gradient on border-box.

Motion. --motion-fast 120ms and --motion-base 220ms on --ease-standard (cubic-bezier(.2, .7, .2, 1)), defined in components/bundle.css because tokens hold no motion. Motion answers a person: pressing, opening, switching tabs. Two ambient exceptions, both still under reduced motion: the running status pulse and the light travelling along a loading or running channel. Never animate the Relief.

The Relief. The motif in full: routed ridges, like contour lines pressed into slate. Each ridge has a crisp 1.25px crest in color-sheen, a lit flank tapering away toward the top left, a shaded flank in color-halo tapering toward the bottom right, and a fine shaded lip under the crest. The flanks are stacks of strokes whose widths grow on a square curve, so the profile peaks sharply at the crest and tails off softly, with darker valleys between ridges. Flank strength is set per theme in components/bundle.css (--bn-r-lit, --bn-r-shade, --bn-r-lip). Ridges dissolve toward every edge of their box. Two routes: route (concentric ridges turning down, with a hairpin) in every PageHeader, and hairpin (one U-turn) in EmptyState. PageHeader gives it its own column beside the text, hidden below 900px, so it never sits under text. Keep it decorative (aria-hidden), at most once per view, never animated.

Accessibility. WCAG 2.2 AA throughout, checked in both themes across every component (1,225 text elements each) with axe-core and a pixel-level contrast check for text on gradients and bezels. - Text contrast: 4.5:1 or more for all text, 3:1 or more for large text, on every surface, tint and bezel face. Disabled controls are exempt, as WCAG allows, but still carry their label. - No text over imagery: the Relief has its own column in PageHeader and sits above, never behind, text in EmptyState. The cover's name sits on plain ground. - Sizes: nothing under 12px. Body is 15px, hints and errors 13px because they carry instructions, Mono never smaller than 12px. Charts keep a 560px minimum width and scroll sideways rather than shrink their 12px labels. - Structure: grids that present tables (TurnTimeline, CompareView) carry table, row, row-header and cell roles. Every chart has a text summary and RateChart pairs with RateTable. - Interaction: every control has a visible label; focus is a 2px color-focus outline; Tabs move with arrow keys; highlights open with Enter; selected tabs and the current nav item change weight as well as fill. Long transcripts stay readable at 200% zoom.

Iconography

Agora has no icon set or logo yet. The name is set in plain type (Instrument Sans, 640 weight, 86% width) beside a single accent bead; replace it when a real mark exists. Glyphs are drawn inline as small SVG at 10px, stroked in currentColor:

  • Status: queued (ring), running (pulsing bead), succeeded (tick), failed (cross), cancelled (dash), stopped at budget (two bars).
  • Trust: trusted (filled bead), provisional (half bead), retired (bead with a slash, struck text), unrated (dashed ring).
  • ProfileStrip markers: ✓ expected and found, – expected but missing, ! expected absent but found, ⚑ judges differ by two points or more. Each has a text equivalent for screen readers.

Intentional additions

Beyond the brief's inventory: the Relief component, which carries the visual language; tokens color-border-strong, color-channel, color-rim, color-shade, color-halo, color-sheen, color-bezel-hi, color-bezel-lo, radius-pill, shadow-raise and shadow-inset, which the channel, the bezels and the Relief need. Every other token and component name matches the brief, so tokens.css can replace web/styles/tokens.css without code changes.

Foundations

Button

Dark
Light

Raised pill for actions; primary for the one action a view is for, secondary for the rest.

Provide children (a verb that says what happens: "Start audit of 12 samples", never "Submit"), variant (primary or secondary, the default), onClick or type, and disabled.

  • The outline is a bezel: lit by color-bezel-hi on top, shaded by color-bezel-lo below, over a face that is lighter on top. Primary mixes its bezel from color-accent.
  • Hovering brightens the bezel toward color-sheen. Pressing flips bezel and face and sinks the button into shadow-inset, the one place the motif answers a hand.
  • One primary per view. Keep labels in sentence case.
  • Don't put icons alone in a button; the label carries the meaning.

LinkButton

Dark
Light

An action that reads as a link, such as "Download the Inspect log".

Provide children and either href (renders a link) or onClick (renders a button that looks like a link).

  • Use for secondary, low-weight actions inside text, tables and panels.
  • Underlined in color-accent, so it reads as a link inside dense tables.

Field

Dark
Light

A labelled text input or text area, set into a shallow well.

Provide label, and optionally hint, error, multiline, id, plus any input props (value, onChange, placeholder).

  • Every field has a visible label; hints sit under the control, errors replace nothing and say how to fix the problem.
  • The well (color-channel, shadow-inset) has a color-border-strong edge so the control meets 3:1.

Select

Dark
Light

A labelled native select in the same well as Field.

Provide label, options (strings or {value, label}), and optionally hint, error, value, onChange.

  • Native control: keyboard and screen readers work as people expect.
  • Use for choices of five or more; Radio for fewer.

Checkbox

Dark
Light

A labelled checkbox: a small well that fills with a raised accent bead when checked.

Provide label, and optionally hint, checked or defaultChecked, onChange, disabled.

Radio

Dark
Light

A labelled radio button; group several with one name.

Provide label, name, value, and optionally hint, checked, onChange. Wrap a group in a fieldset with a legend.

Tabs

Dark
Light

A groove of tabs with a raised lozenge on the selected one; Review's three queues use it.

Provide tabs ({id, label, count?}), label (what the set is), and either panels (an object of id to content) or value with onChange.

  • Arrow keys, Home and End move between tabs.
  • Counts sit beside the label in tabular figures.

Dialog

Dark
Light

A confirmation over the page, such as cancelling a run.

Provide title, children (what will happen), actions (buttons, the safe one first), open, and onClose (Escape calls it). inline renders it in place, for previews.

  • Title asks the question; the primary action repeats its verb: "Cancel run", not "OK".
  • Uses shadow-1, one of only two places elevation appears.

Toast

Dark
Light

A short confirmation that says what happened: "Verdict recorded".

Provide children, and optionally tone (ok, the default, warn, danger, info) and action (a LinkButton such as "Undo").

  • Past tense, the same verb as the action that caused it.
  • Uses shadow-1; dismisses itself, so never the only place an error appears.

Notice

Dark
Light

An inline message: info for context, danger for errors.

Provide children, and optionally title and tone (info, the default, or danger).

  • Errors say what went wrong and how to fix it, without apology.
  • A bead marks the tone, so meaning never rests on colour alone; no coloured side borders.

Card

Dark
Light

A titled section of a page, on color-surface with a lit top edge. Not every block needs one.

Provide title, children, and optionally actions.

  • Use tone and spacing to group before reaching for a card; avoid grids of identical cards.

PageHeader

Dark
Light

Title, a short intro and an optional quiet line, with the Relief motif in its own column on the right. The motif never sits under text, and hides below 900px.

Provide title, and optionally intro, note (the quiet line, such as Lens's fixed wording), area (the page's area, for analytics and future variation; every area draws the same route today) and actions.

  • This is where the channel motif is boldest; keep everything else on the page quiet.
  • Lens pages must pass note: "Expressed disposition, read from text. Lens does not see inside the model."

Badge

Dark
Light

A short status word in a pill: neutral, ok, warn or danger.

Provide children and optionally tone and icon.

  • One or two words. Pair tone with a word or glyph; never colour alone.

Mono

Dark
Light

Monospace for hashes, IDs and model names, nothing else.

Provide children, and optionally truncate (characters to keep; the full value shows on hover) and plain (no fill, for tables and lists).

AppShell

Dark
Light

The frame of every page: the Agora wordmark, the navigation groove and the signed-in person, over a centred main column.

Provide current (the area's label), children, and optionally user ({name, role}), items and onNavigate.

  • The main column grows to content-width (72rem) with space-6 gutters.
  • There is no logo yet: the name is set in plain type beside a single accent bead.

Nav

Dark
Light

The top navigation: eight areas in a groove, the current one raised.

Provide current, and optionally items ({label, href}) and onNavigate. The default items are Home, Agents, Explore, Measure, Lens, Review, Reports and Settings.

Data

DataTable

Dark
Light

A sortable table with a sticky header, tabular figures and two densities.

Provide columns ({key, label, align?, render?, sortValue?, sortable?}), rows, and optionally density (comfortable or dense), caption, initialSort ({key, dir}), rowHeaders and maxHeight.

  • Right-align numbers. Links stay underlined so they read as links in dense rows.

ScoreCell

Dark
Light

A judge score with a small mark showing where it sits and how the samples spread; links to its evidence.

Provide value, and optionally max (10), samples (other repetitions' scores), href, label, threshold (7) and concern (false when high is good).

  • A score at or over the threshold takes a color-danger bead; others color-chart-1. The number always shows.

Heatmap

Dark
Light

Judge dimensions by transcript: our custom dimensions first, Petri's built-in ones folded away. Each cell is a ScoreCell over a cool steel tint (color-chart-1) that deepens with the score.

Provide dimensions and builtIn ({key, label}), and transcripts ({id, label, seed?, href, scores: {key: {value, samples?, href?}}}).

  • The tint never passes 28% of color-chart-1, so the score and its "of 10" keep 4.5:1. High scores also take a color-danger bead, so concern never rests on the tint alone.
  • Order rows worst first when the view supports it.

RateChart

Dark
Light

Elicitation rate by prompt version: each version a bead on the route, a whisker for the spread across repetitions, and its repetitions as small rings. A version that got worse turns color-danger, gains a ring and says by how much.

Provide data ({version, rate, low, high, reps, worse?} in order), and optionally title and max.

  • Always pair with RateTable for screen readers and exports.

RateTable

Dark
Light

The RateChart's numbers as a table, for screen readers and exports.

Provide data (the same shape as RateChart) and optionally caption.

IntervalBar

Dark
Light

A share and its 95% interval on a 0 to 100% channel.

Provide value, low, high, and optionally label and family (tints the band and bead with a Lens family).

Runs

StatusBadge

Dark
Light

A run or sample status, with a glyph for each so status never rests on colour.

Provide status: queued, running, succeeded, failed, cancelled or stopped at budget.

  • Running pulses gently and stops pulsing when reduced motion is on.

RunProgress

Dark
Light

Live counts by status while a run goes: beads filling a channel, with a legend in words.

Provide counts (by status), and optionally total and unit (samples, or rollouts for Measure).

Provenance

Dark
Light

Everything that makes a run reproducible, in one list: status, prompt, model per role, mode, route, hashes, taxonomy version, engines, commit, tokens against budget, cost, times and the Inspect log.

Provide run; any field left out is skipped.

  • Hashes truncate with the full value on hover. Model names stay in Mono.

Transcripts

MessageView

Dark
Light

One message on the conversation's route: a channel runs down the left and each message is a node on it. The system prompt and tool results fold away; the member is "played by the auditor"; tool calls sit under the agent's message.

Provide role (system, member, agent or tool), children, and optionally author, index (for M3 references), toolCalls ({name, args}) and thinking (summary or full).

  • Stack MessageViews directly; the route joins up between them.
  • Keep the reading column under 46rem.

Cited

Dark
Light

Judge text where each citation, such as [M3], becomes a link to the message it cites.

Provide text, and optionally hrefFor(n) (defaults to #m<n>).

ContentNote

Dark
Light

A quiet flag before distressing content, with a way to change who sees it.

Provide topic (lower case, such as "self harm"), and optionally href and linkText.

FullThinking

Dark
Light

Full thinking, when Anthropic grants it: closed by default, admins and compliance only, each reading audited, never shown beside summaries.

Provide children, and optionally canRead (false shows a restricted notice instead) and onOpen (record the reading).

Lens

LabelChip

Dark
Light

A Lens label: its family's hue as a bead, then the label name, optionally with its trust.

Provide family (care, collaboration, judgement, curiosity or risk), label, and optionally trust and agreement.

  • The name always shows; the hue only groups.

TrustBadge

Dark
Light

How far experts and judges agree on a label: trusted (0.8 or more), provisional (from 0.667), retired (below) or unrated (too few expert ratings). Each has its own glyph.

Provide trust and optionally agreement.

Highlighted

Dark
Light

The phrase highlight: a tint of the label's family, its strength set by the panel's median intensity. Hover or focus shows the label and intensity; choosing it opens the FindingPane.

Provide family, label, median (0 to 3, halves allowed), children (the quoted phrase), and optionally judges, selected and onSelect.

  • Where phrases overlap, render only the strongest.

ProfileStrip

Dark
Light

One cell per label: the observed intensity fills a channel, with the expected band lit faintly behind. Markers: ✓ expected and found, – expected but missing, ! expected absent but found, ⚑ judges differ by two points or more.

Provide cells ({label, family, observed, expected: {kind: 'present' | 'absent' | 'either', min?, max?}, disputed?}) and optionally label.

FindingPane

Dark
Light

The chosen label in full: definition, trust, the panel's median, and each judge's intensity, quotes and rationale, including quotes dropped because they were not in the reply.

Provide label, family, median, judges ({model, intensity, quotes, dropped?, rationale}), and optionally definition, trust, agreement and onClose.

TurnTimeline

Dark
Light

How each label rises and settles across a conversation's replies: one channel per label, a bead per reply sized by intensity.

Provide rows ({label, family, values}, one value per reply) and optionally replies and label.

CompareView

Dark
Light
